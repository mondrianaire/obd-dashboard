## Result
**T-0001 report: thermal-order FAIL on OBD Fusion 2026-09-01 12:58**

**Which rule fires.** Only the lower bound, `ic < amb-15`.
- It fires on 249 of 301 rows (82.7%). The upper bound, `ic > iat+5`, fires on 0 rows.
- Command: a scratchpad script that loads `telemetry_data.json` plus `drives/<id>.json` and re-applies the C5 conditions. It skips rows with amb<10 or iat<10, as the test does.
- Distribution in this drive (303 rows):
  - amb is about 91–95 °F, median 93.2.
  - icTempF is 72–80 °F, median 75.
  - iat is 89.6–104 °F, median 95.
  - ic sits about 18 °F below ambient, and the gap shrinks slowly. It is 72 against 91.4 at t=25 s and 80 against 95 at t=275 s.
  - Coolant climbs from about 100 °F to 205 °F over the drive, so this is a warm-up drive.
- Of the 249 violations, the 52 that pass are the late rows where ic has crept up to within 15 °F of ambient.

**Cause: check too strict for a cold-soaked start.** I don't think this is an ingest fault.
- **Not a mapping or unit error.** The channels are in the same °F range as the passing drives. `prep_drives.py:233` writes `icTempF`, and line 379 labels it as the user's custom Mode-22 PID. Nothing about this drive looks different at ingest. The IAT-to-IC ordering is normal, with ic below iat throughout.
- **A thermal-lag pattern.**
  - The car started cold, with coolant around 100 °F.
  - The ic reading begins well under ambient and rises about 8 °F in 5 minutes. That is what a sensor reads when it was soaked cool (a parked, shaded or air-conditioned spot) and is catching up.
  - The ambient channel reads about 91–95 °F.
  - I infer this from the numbers. I can't prove which sensor is lagging without the raw CSV.
- **The next drive is the same trip.** The 13:07 drive has ic of 83–93 against amb 93.2, which is within 15 °F, and it passes. That fits the warm-up explanation.
- **Not a genuine fault.** ic is far below iat and nowhere near an over-temperature condition.

**Recommendation: adjust the check.** Skip warm-up rows in C5, for example `coolant < 160` (or the first N seconds). A drive that is only warm-up would then have too few rows and skip (`total < 30`).
